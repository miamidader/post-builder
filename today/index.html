PORTMIAMI RANKS AS THE BUSIEST CRUISE PORT IN THE WORLD

PortMiami holds the top spot as the busiest cruise port in the entire world. Right here in our local waters, the facility leads the globe in cruise passenger traffic. In 2017, the port handled a total of 5,340,559 cruise passengers. That historic volume of 5,340,559 travelers solidifies PortMiami as the world's busiest cruise port by passenger traffic.
