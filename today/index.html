What began nearly fifteen years ago in Miami Beach with just six electric carts has grown into a massive regional transit service. University of Miami graduates Jason Spiegel and Kris Kimball originally created Freebee as an ad-supported ride service, giving free trips around South Beach and Brickell before launching an on-demand app in 2015.

A partnership with Key Biscayne in 2017 pivoted the company toward government contracts. Today, Freebee runs over 300 vehicles across more than 50 communities, providing over two million free neighborhood rides each year. Following a merger last month with autonomous vehicle company Beep, the combined operation will employ nearly 1,000 workers across more than 60 communities.
