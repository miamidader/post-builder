Miami-Dade Fire Rescue is seeking county approval to spend an extra $2.9 million to maintain its Air Rescue fleet. Excessive corrosion driven by local humidity and saltwater intrusion has accelerated wear on the helicopters, which were bought in November 2019. Assistant Fire Chief Rick Rojas explained that Miami's coastal climate creates unique repair challenges for the department's four Leonardo AW139 helicopters. Unplanned radio work and aircraft incidents have depleted the bureau's original $6.66 million budget faster than expected. The county's Safety and Health Committee unanimously approved the resolution, which raises the repair allocation to $9.56 million. A full commission vote is scheduled for January 20.
