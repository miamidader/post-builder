Miami-Dade County lost 10,115 residents between July 2024 and July 2025, recording the third-largest population drop among all counties nationwide. The local population slipped from 2.81 million to 2.80 million people. Domestic departures fueled the shift. Over 72,000 more residents relocated to other parts of the country than moved into the county, marking the highest domestic migration loss in Florida. While about 54,200 international newcomers arrived, that pace dropped from more than 78,000 during the prior year. Living expenses remain a primary strain across the region. Sixty percent of middle-income renters pay over 30 percent of their income toward housing, while purchasing a typical home now demands almost double the median household income.
