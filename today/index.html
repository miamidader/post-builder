HISTORIC FREEZES DESTROYED NORTH FLORIDA CROPS WHILE MIAMI CROPS SURVIVED

A freeze in December 1894 and a second one on February 7, 1895 destroyed crops across northern Florida. In our area, Miami's crops survived both freezes. Julia Tuttle used the survival of Miami's crops to convince railroad tycoon Henry Flagler to extend his Florida East Coast Railway south. This became known as the freeze that made Miami. While cold destroyed crops across northern Florida, Miami's surviving crops allowed Tuttle to convince Flagler to bring his railway south to our community.
