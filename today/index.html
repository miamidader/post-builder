Our area traces its formal beginnings back to February 4, 1836, the day Dade County was officially established. The county earned its historic identity from Major Francis L. Dade as well as the Miami River flowing through the land.

The year 1836 also brought key defensive developments to the territory. Construction began on Fort Dallas, which was erected that very year to serve as a military base.
