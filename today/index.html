FLORIDA CROCODILE DIES OF APPARENT LEAD POISONING IN FIRST-KNOWN CASE

A crocodile has died of apparent lead poisoning in Florida, marking the first-known case of its kind in the state. The tragic death represents an unprecedented occurrence for Florida wildlife. Prior to this incident, there had been no known cases of a crocodile dying from apparent lead poisoning anywhere across the state. This reported fatality stands as Florida's first-known case of a crocodile death linked to apparent lead poisoning.
