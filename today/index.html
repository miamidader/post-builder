FIRST TRAIN ARRIVED IN MIAMI ON APRIL 13, 1896 WITH HENRY FLAGLER

A defining moment in Miami history took place on April 13, 1896, when the first train officially arrived here. Henry Flagler was aboard that historic train as it made its entrance into Miami. This milestone set the stage for what was to come later that year. Just months after the train arrived, Miami was officially incorporated as a city on July 28, 1896. From the arrival of Henry Flagler on April 13, 1896, to the official city incorporation on July 28, 1896, these key dates marked the earliest days of our community.
