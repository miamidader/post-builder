In response, the Mosquito Control Division of the Department of Solid Waste Management is deploying truck spray treatments using Bti larvicide alongside adult mosquito spraying. County technicians monitor more than 350 traps every week, while partnering with the Florida Department of Health, the University of Florida, and Florida International University on viral tracking and testing.

Officials urge residents to empty standing water, as species such as Aedes aegypti require only one ounce of water to breed. Property inspections can be requested by calling 311.
