MIA RANKS AS NUMBER ONE U.S. AIRPORT FOR INTERNATIONAL CARGO

Miami International Airport stands as a major hub for global transit. MIA moves more international cargo than any other airport across the United States. In addition, the facility holds the record as the largest U.S. airport for international cargo operations. On the traveler side, Miami International Airport is also the third largest U.S. airport for international passengers. Together, these milestones solidify MIA as the premier domestic gateway moving the highest volume of international cargo nationwide while simultaneously serving as the third biggest hub for international passengers.
