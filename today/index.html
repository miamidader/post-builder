THE HIGHEST POINT IN MIAMI-DADE IS ONLY 20 TO 26 FEET

Here in Miami-Dade, the local landscape is defined by its low elevation. The county's highest point reaches only about 20 to 26 feet up from the ground. This means the highest point across all of Miami-Dade sits just 20 to 26 feet above sea level. On the opposite end, the lowest point in the county is at sea level along the Atlantic Ocean. Between the waters of the Atlantic Ocean and the highest point in Miami-Dade, the entire county spans an elevation change of only 20 to 26 feet.
