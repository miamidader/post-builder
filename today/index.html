MAN ARRESTED AFTER FLOWER DELIVERY DISPUTE TURNS VIOLENT AT MIAMI-DADE FUNERAL HOME

A flower delivery turned violent Thursday afternoon outside Mitchell Funeral Home at 8080 Northwest 22nd Avenue in northwest Miami-Dade.

Authorities arrested 51-year-old Hollywood resident Michael Keith Koehler after an argument erupted when a 73-year-old employee explained the business no longer accepts floral arrangements.

Surveillance footage showed Koehler advancing onto the property, ramming his shoulder into the senior staff member, and hitting another person in the face with a phone. A victim restrained Koehler in a headlock until Miami-Dade deputies arrived.

After receiving treatment at North Shore Hospital for cuts and scrapes, Koehler was booked into the Turner Guilford Knight Correctional Center on battery charges, including battery on someone 65 or older.
