MIAMI-DADE IS THE ONLY U.S. COUNTY WITH A HYPHENATED NAME

Our local area holds a truly unique title across the entire country. Miami-Dade is officially the only county in the United States with a hyphenated name. This unique status began on November 13, 1997. On that day, Dade County was renamed Miami-Dade County. With that name change, it became the only county in the nation to feature a hyphenated name. Out of every county across the United States, Miami-Dade County stands completely alone with this distinctive hyphen in its official name.
