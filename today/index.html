Miami-Dade commissioners voted unanimously on Tuesday to advance a 100 million dollar redevelopment proposal for the shuttered Seaquarium site. The county-owned, 38-acre waterfront property closed down last year following an eviction battle centered on animal-welfare concerns.

Developer David Martin of Terra plans to lease the land for 99 years, keeping the Seaquarium name while building an aquarium, public baywalk, restaurants, event venues, a 325-slip marina, and drydock storage for over 500 boats.

Environmental advocates urged officials to delay the action. Rachel Silverstein of Miami Waterkeeper raised alarms over water quality, coral, and seagrass damage. Martin stated his design reduces environmental impact on Biscayne Bay. A final commission vote is expected by the end of the year.
