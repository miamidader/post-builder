The Lucy Project, led by founder Sandra Bermudez, is expanding into five additional elementary campuses this year after driving dramatic academic gains at Norwood Elementary in Miami Gardens. At Norwood, kindergarten reading proficiency surged from 52 percent to 88 percent in two years.

Backed by a $500,000 donation from the Partnership for Miami, the program now reaches nine campuses, including Riverside, Broadmoor, Melrose, James H. Bright, and Laura C. Sanders elementary schools, plus Liberty Academy for pre-K.

Costing approximately $1,500 per kindergartner, the organization uses evidence-based phonics instruction to assist local schools where third-grade proficiency rates sit under 55 percent.
