SEPTEMBER 1926 CATEGORY 4 HURRICANE LEFT THOUSANDS HOMELESS IN MIAMI

In September 1926, our area faced immense destruction when the Great Miami Hurricane struck. The powerful Category 4 storm hit the community with devastating force. The impact on the local population was catastrophic. The storm caused about 373 deaths across the area. In the aftermath of the disaster, between 25,000 and 50,000 people were left homeless as a result of the destruction. Today, the Great Miami Hurricane of September 1926 remains an important part of our local history, marking a defining moment for the entire region.
