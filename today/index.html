Miami-Dade property owners can now pay their property taxes using bitcoin. Tax Collector Dariel Fernandez announced Monday that taxpayers can use cryptocurrency online around the clock or in person across 13 county offices and T-Connect mobile units. A third-party vendor instantly converts every bitcoin transaction into U.S. dollars before the funds reach the county. Because of this conversion, Miami-Dade does not hold, invest in, or manage digital currency, while still receiving full dollar payments. The new option arrives during Florida's property tax collection season. Owners who submit payments by Nov. 30 receive a 4% early-payment discount under the county's Save the Max campaign before discounts decrease in December.
