MIAMI-DADE LEADS FLORIDA WITH THE STATE'S BIGGEST ECONOMY

Miami-Dade County officially holds the biggest economy in Florida.

Economic data highlights the sheer scale of the county, showing that Miami-Dade County reached a GDP of $239.652 billion in 2023. This total was the largest gross domestic product recorded by any county across the entire state of Florida.

With a $239.652 billion GDP, Miami-Dade stands at the very top of Florida. The local economy is the largest in the state, surpassing every other Florida county in overall economic size for 2023.
