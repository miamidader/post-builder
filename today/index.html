MIAMI RANKS AS THE THIRD-LARGEST SKYLINE IN THE ENTIRE UNITED STATES

Our city stands tall on the national stage. Miami now officially holds the third-largest skyline in the United States, driven by a dense and soaring vertical landscape. Across the urban core, Miami features more than 300 high-rises. Of that massive collection of buildings, about 70 of them stand taller than 492 feet. These towering structures elevate our local skyline, securing Miami's position with the third-largest skyline in the country. With more than 300 high-rises and approximately 70 rising past the 492-foot mark, the city continues to maintain its major national standing.
