OVER HALF OF MIAMI-DADE RESIDENTS LIVE OUTSIDE ANY INCORPORATED CITY

Living in Miami-Dade does not always mean living inside an official city. Miami-Dade has 34 incorporated cities, yet over half of Miami-Dade residents live outside any city. As of 2013, about 52 percent of residents lived in unincorporated areas. Even with 34 incorporated cities across the county, more than half of the population in Miami-Dade lives in unincorporated communities rather than inside city limits.
