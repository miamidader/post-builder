MIAMI-DADE IS THE MOST POPULOUS MAJORITY-HISPANIC COUNTY IN AMERICA

Miami-Dade holds the distinction of being the most Hispanic county in America. In 2020, 68.73 percent of Miami-Dade residents were Hispanic, solidifying our county as the single most populous majority-Hispanic county in the country. The presence of Hispanic culture is reflected throughout our community, where about 66.5 percent of residents speak Spanish at home. Furthermore, international origins define a major portion of our local population, with 54 percent of Miami-Dade residents born outside the U.S.
