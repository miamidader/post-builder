Miami-Dade County voters must renew their vote-by-mail ballot requests to receive ballots for the 2027 and 2028 election cycles, according to Miami-Dade County Supervisor of Elections Alina Garcia. This includes voting in the 2028 presidential election. Under Florida Statute 101.62, state law requires mail ballot requests to be renewed after every regularly scheduled general election cycle. Current requests remain valid through December 31, 2026. Voters who requested ballots for 2026 elections must submit a new request to continue receiving them through December 31, 2028. Renewing a vote-by-mail request does not alter a resident's voter registration status. Election officials encourage voters to renew early to stay prepared.
