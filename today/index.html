MIAMI DADE COLLEGE RANKS SECOND NATIONWIDE WITH OVER 100,000 UNDERGRADUATES

The Miami area is home to some of the largest higher education institutions in the nation. Miami Dade College stands as one of the biggest in America, holding the second largest undergraduate enrollment of any college in the United States. Today, the institution educates over 100,000 students across its community.

Local student numbers reach national scale at multiple campuses. Florida International University also earned high placement across the country, ranking as the fifth largest university nationwide by enrollment as of 2020.

Together, both institutions demonstrate the remarkable size and reach of higher education enrollment right here in the Miami area.
