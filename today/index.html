NEARLY A QUARTER OF MIAMI-DADE COUNTY IS COVERED BY WATER

Miami-Dade County covers a total of 2,431 square miles across our area. While residents spend their daily routines on land, a significant portion of our county territory consists of water. Specifically, 532 square miles of Miami-Dade are made up of water. That amounts to 21.9 percent of the entire county. In total, nearly a quarter of Miami-Dade County is composed of water, accounting for 532 of the area's 2,431 square miles.
